Coin Farming

Efficient Coin Farming

A full breakdown of the priority actions — and the reasoning behind them — for maximizing the coins you take home from Pokajan.

📌

The short answer: efficient coin farming comes down to combining three multiplier factors — your final rank, your BET multiplier, and the Holo Passport.

Coin Factors

What Affects the Coins You Earn

The coins you ultimately take home from Pokajan are decided by several multiplier factors stacked together. Let's start with the big picture.

Factor Effect Type Details
Final Rank Multiplier Multiplier ×2.5 for 1st, ×1.5 for 2nd, ×1 for 3rd–4th. Your placement directly determines your reward.
BET Multiplier Multiplier Betting more coins tends to raise your expected value (one analysis reported as much as +86%).
Holo Passport Multiplier (Paid) A monthly-subscription paid item. When your match result is a net gain, your earned coins are doubled (no effect when you're at a net loss).
