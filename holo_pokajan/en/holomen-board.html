Coin Farming

Efficient Coin Farming

A full breakdown of the priority actions — and the reasoning behind them — for maximizing the coins you take home from Pokajan.

📌

The short answer: efficient coin farming comes down to combining three multiplier factors — your final rank, your BET multiplier, and the Holo Passport.

Coin Factors

What Affects the Coins You Earn

The coins you ultimately take home from Pokajan are decided by several multiplier factors stacked together. Let's start with the big picture.

Factor Effect Type Details
Final Rank Multiplier Multiplier ×2.5 for 1st, ×1.5 for 2nd, ×1 for 3rd–4th. Your placement directly determines your reward.
BET Multiplier Multiplier Betting more coins tends to raise your expected value (one analysis reported as much as +86%).
Holo Passport Multiplier (Paid) A monthly-subscription paid item. When your match result is a net gain, your earned coins are doubled (no effect when you're at a net loss).
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about coin farming.

Q. What's the single most important multiplier?

Of the three, the final rank multiplier has the biggest impact. The gap between 1st (×2.5) and 4th (×1) is a full 2.5x, so if you want to grow your coins efficiently, prioritizing a higher finish — see the Strategy & Safe Tiles page — matters most.

Q. How much do the multipliers add up to when combined?

For example, if you finish 1st (×2.5) and also have the Holo Passport's net-gain bonus (×2) active, you'd earn 5x (2.5×2) your normal coins. Since multiple multipliers stack multiplicatively, being mindful of rank, BET, and Holo Passport at the same time compounds your gains.

Q. Should I bet high?

Analysis suggests betting more coins tends to raise your expected value. Of course, that comes with the tradeoff of bigger losses when you lose, so the basic approach is to bet aggressively when you have coins to spare.

Q. Is the Holo Passport worth subscribing to?

It's a monthly-subscription item that doubles your earned coins only when your match result is a net gain (no effect when you're at a net loss). The more you play Pokajan, the more benefit you'll get from it.
