About

What is Pokajan?

"Pokajan" is a 4-player, multiplayer-enabled mini-game you can play in hololive Dreams (Holodori). Using special "Pokajan!" cards, you form yaku by matching Holomen or their generation/unit, and fight over each other's coins. The rules resemble mahjong or Donjara, but the biggest twist is that the game doesn't end when someone completes a hand — play keeps going, with as many wins as possible, until the deck runs out. See the Rules & How to Play page for the full breakdown.
